RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date"),
between Maple Ridge Properties LLC, referred to herein as the "Lessor", and Jordan T. Ellison,
referred to herein as the "Lessee", for the property located at 48 Birchwood Lane, Unit 3B,
Springvale (the "Premises").

ARTICLE 1 — TERM
1.1 The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on April 1, 2026 and continue for a
    period of twelve (12) months, ending March 31, 2027 (the "Initial Term"), unless earlier
    terminated in accordance with Article 6.

ARTICLE 2 — PAYMENTS
2.1 The Lessee shall pay to the Lessor a Base Rent (also referred to in local practice as
    "Kaltmiete" or Cold Rent) of $1,350.00 per calendar month, due on the first day of each
    month.
2.2 In addition to the Base Rent, the Lessee shall reimburse the Lessor for Additional Costs,
    covering heating, water, waste disposal, and building maintenance, currently estimated at
    $275.00 per month. Additional Costs are billed together with Base Rent and are subject to
    annual reconciliation against actual costs incurred.
2.3 The Base Rent shall be adjusted annually according to the Consumer Price Index, but no
    increase in Base Rent shall take effect before the fourteenth month of occupancy.
2.4 Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance.

ARTICLE 3 — BOND
3.1 Prior to occupancy, the Lessee shall pay to the Lessor a Bond equivalent to two (2) times
    the Base Rent then in effect, to be held as security for the performance of the Lessee's
    obligations under this Agreement.
3.2 The Bond shall be deposited into a separate escrow account and shall not accrue interest
    for the benefit of the Lessee, except where required by applicable law.
3.3 Within thirty (30) days following the end of the Initial Term (or any renewal thereof),
    the Lessor shall return the Bond, less any deductions for unpaid amounts owed under this
    Agreement or damage to the Premises beyond normal wear and tear.

ARTICLE 4 — USE OF PREMISES
4.1 The Premises shall be used solely as a private residence for the Lessee and immediate
    family members disclosed at signing.
4.2 No subletting, assignment, or short-term rental of the Premises is permitted without the
    prior written consent of the Lessor.

ARTICLE 5 — MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
5.1 The Lessor shall be responsible for maintaining the structural elements, plumbing, and
    electrical systems of the Premises in good working order.
5.2 The Lessee shall promptly notify the Lessor of any defects or needed repairs and shall
    not undertake structural alterations without prior written consent.

ARTICLE 6 — KÜNDIGUNGSFRIST (TERMINATION NOTICE)
6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement upon expiry of the Initial Term by providing
    the other party with written notice no later than three (3) full calendar months prior
    to the intended termination date (the "Kündigungsfrist").
6.2 In the event of a material breach by the Lessee, including non-payment of Base Rent or
    Additional Costs for a period exceeding thirty (30) days, the Lessor may terminate this
    Agreement upon fourteen (14) days' written notice, notwithstanding Section 6.1.
6.3 Notice under this Article must be delivered in writing, either by registered mail or by
    hand delivery with written acknowledgment of receipt.

ARTICLE 7 — GENERAL PROVISIONS
7.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes
    all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements, whether oral or written.
7.2 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which the Premises
    is located.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
